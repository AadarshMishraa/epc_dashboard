# EPC industry news

Goal: tell Aadarsh what changed in the last 7 days in Indian solar EPC and distribution that affects what we **build** (features, compliance) or **sell** (pitch, pricing, timing). Not a news dump.

## Scope (in order of weight)
1. **Policy and subsidy:** MNRE notices, PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana rooftop subsidy and portal changes, PM-KUSUM, ALMM List-I (modules) and List-II (cells) dates and exemptions, customs and anti-dumping duty, GST rate or invoicing rules, e-invoicing thresholds.
2. **Grid and DISCOM rules:** net-metering and gross-metering changes, Electricity (Rights of Consumers) rules, state regulator orders (start with Maharashtra, MSEDCL, then other big rooftop states), rooftop approval timelines.
3. **Prices and supply:** module, cell, inverter, battery and polysilicon price moves, shortages, major manufacturer capacity or listing news, import and BIS or quality rules.
4. **Money:** green loans, bank and NBFC rooftop schemes, RBI or MNRE financing changes.
5. **Competitors and tools:** EPC software, lead-gen and rooftop-installer platforms, funding or product launches that overlap what we are building.
6. **Rules that touch our software:** DPDP Act and Rules milestones, CERT-In, WhatsApp or messaging policy changes that affect installer workflows.

## Method
1. Search each scope item for the last 7 days (use today's date; never an old year in a query). Prefer primary sources (PIB and MNRE releases, regulator orders, gazette notices) and trade press (Mercom India, ET EnergyWorld, PV Magazine India, Saur Energy, Reuters). Open the page before stating a number, date or rule.
2. Keep only items that changed something. Drop recycled commentary, opinion pieces, and anything older than 7 days unless it is a deadline that is now close.
3. Never invent. If a claim rests on one weak source, label it "single source". If nothing material happened in a section, say "quiet".
4. Do not reproduce article text. Summarise in your own words, one line each.

## Output (short, strong point of view)
- **Top 3** items that change what we build or sell, each: what happened, the date, **so what for us** in one line, link.
- **By theme:** up to 7 more one-liners with date and link, grouped under the scope headings above. Skip empty themes.
- **Deadlines coming:** dated items in the next 90 days (ALMM, subsidy cut-offs, rule effective dates).
- **Watch next:** 2 items to check next week.
- Hard cap about 400 words. No filler, no hedging, no "it is important to note".

When run inside this repo, map each Top-3 "so what" to a product impact if one exists (a feature in `docs/prd/01-prd.md`, a rule in `docs/compliance/register.md`) and say so; if it needs a decision, name it.

## Rules
- Informational only: not legal, tax or investment advice.
- Quote at most one short phrase per source; link instead.
- Do not send email or messages and do not post anywhere. Deliver the brief in the session.
